Privacy Policy — PartyHub -SR Events

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

PartyHub -SR Events ("we", "our", or "us") operates the PartyHub -SR Events mobile application and web platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect customer information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Verification Data: Customer mobile phone number used for One-Time Password (OTP) authentication and customer name.
  • Event & Booking Details: Selected occasion, event date, time slot (morning/afternoon), venue address within Annamayya and Kadapa districts, selected decoration/event services, and digital e-agreement signatures.
  • Payment Records: Payment installment status (20% booking advance, 70% event day, 10% completion) and transaction reference IDs. We do not store raw UPI PINs, bank passwords, or card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account via OTP and display personalized service catalogs and pricing.
  • To schedule, coordinate, and execute event decorations, special entries, and artist bookings at your venue.
  • To send booking confirmations, balance reminders, and customer support updates via phone or WhatsApp.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or rent customer personal data to third parties. Booking details are accessed strictly by authorized PartyHub -SR Events staff (Rayachoti & Madanapalle branches) to fulfill your event order. All app traffic is encrypted over HTTPS.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request access to or permanent deletion of your account and booking records at any time through our Account & Data Deletion Page or by contacting our support desk directly.
