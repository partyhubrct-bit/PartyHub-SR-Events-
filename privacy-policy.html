Effective Date: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the PartyHub - SR Events mobile application, we collect your name, 10-digit mobile number, event date, selected slot timing, venue address (within Annamayya District and Kadapa District, Andhra Pradesh), digital e-signature, and payment reference details to fulfill event decoration and rental bookings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify customer login via OTP and manage event slot bookings.
  • To coordinate decoration setup, dismantling, and payment milestones (20% advance, 70% setup arrival, 10% completion).
  • To send booking confirmations and balance payment reminders.

3. Data Security & No Third-Party Selling

We do not sell or rent customer personal information to third parties. Data synced to our cloud database is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You may delete your account and personal data anytime inside the app under My Events → Delete My Account & Data, or via our public deletion page: delete-account.html.
